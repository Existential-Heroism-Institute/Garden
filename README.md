# Garden

**Habits that level you up.**

Garden is an iOS app that turns self-development into a world you cultivate:

- **Cultivation** — build daily routines, count habits, and clear to-dos. Every
  completion earns Essence, keeps streaks alive, and fills a calendar you'll
  actually want to look at.
- **Existential Hero Academy** — a curated curriculum of lectures, books, and
  the lives of history's heroes, with quizzes, reflections, and honorary
  certificates to mark the climb.
- **Adventurer's Guild** — the Essence your real-life effort earned becomes XP
  and gear for full D&D 5e characters, complete with dice, spells,
  marketplaces, and optional sync to your own Foundry VTT table.

Everything stays on your device. No accounts, no ads, no tracking.

## Support

Questions, bug reports, and feedback: **help@xheroism.org**

There are no accounts and Garden runs entirely on-device, so there is nothing
we can reset or recover server-side — but we're happy to help with anything
else, and app data can be exported and re-imported from within the app
(Settings → History).
