Garden — Privacy Policy

Last updated 26 August 2026

Garden does not collect your data. There is no account to create, no analytics, no advertising, and no third-party tracking of any kind. What you record in Garden stays on your device unless you deliberately send it somewhere.

What Garden stores, and where

Garden keeps your habits, tasks, cultivation progress, Academy coursework, Adventurer's Guild characters, marketplace purchases and app settings in its own storage on your device. None of it is transmitted to us. We operate no server that receives it, and we could not read it if we wanted to.

This local data is included in your device backups according to your own iOS settings — with one deliberate exception described below.

Your Foundry VTT server password

If you connect Garden to a Foundry Virtual Tabletop server, you may ask Garden to remember that server's password. It is stored in the iOS Keychain, marked as available only on this device while unlocked, and it is never included in a backup and never synced to iCloud. You may additionally require Face ID, Touch ID or your device passcode before it can be read. You can remove it at any time from Settings → Foundry VTT.

Connecting to a Foundry VTT server

Foundry sync sends your character sheet to the server address you enter, and nowhere else. That server is normally your own machine or your game group's, on your own network. We are not a party to that connection, we do not proxy it, and we receive nothing from it. What the operator of that server can see is governed by their arrangements, not ours.

Files you export or import

When you export a character or a shop, Garden hands the file to you through the system share sheet or file picker. Where it goes from there — Files, AirDrop, a messaging app, a cloud drive — is your choice, and the privacy terms of whatever you chose apply to it from that point.

Videos in the Academy

Academy coursework plays videos hosted on YouTube, either in an embedded player inside Garden or by opening YouTube directly. When a video plays, YouTube and Google receive a request from your device and may set cookies and collect information under their own terms, exactly as if you had visited YouTube in a browser. Garden does not receive, store or have access to any of that information. See Google's Privacy Policy.

If you would rather not involve YouTube at all, simply do not open a video lesson; the rest of the app does not contact it.

Notifications

Reminders are scheduled locally on your device. Garden uses no push server, so no notification content ever leaves the device.

What we never do

  • We do not collect, transmit or store your personal data.
  • We do not sell or share data with anyone, because we have none to share.
  • We include no analytics, attribution, advertising or crash-reporting SDK.
  • We do not track you across apps or websites.

Children

Garden is a personal-development and tabletop-companion app suitable for general audiences. Because it collects no personal information from anyone, it collects none from children.

Deleting your data

Deleting Garden from your device removes everything it stored, including the saved Foundry password in the Keychain. There is no copy elsewhere for us to delete on your behalf.
